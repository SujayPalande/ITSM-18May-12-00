Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 07, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Site Engineering ITSM Portal (provided by Cybaemtech) collects, uses, and protects the personal and professional data of its users, including field engineers, administrators, and clients.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the ITSM Portal, we collect certain information to provide and maintain our services:

  • Personal Details: Name, professional email address, and job title/role (Engineer, Admin, HR, Client).
  • Attendance Data: Time and dates of your check-in and check-out events, synchronized with integrated systems (including Zoho).
  • Work Reports: Daily work reports, site-specific updates, and ticket resolutions submitted through the portal.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and technical troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information collected is used for internal business purposes only:

  • To track workforce attendance and manage leave requests.
  • To assign engineers to specific client sites (Assignments).
  • To generate performance reports and helpdesk ticket metrics.
  • To send automated email notifications (e.g., missed check-ins, leave approvals) to authorized roles.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data is highly confidential and is never sold to third parties. It is shared only in the following contexts:

  • Internal Access: Authorized Admin and HR personnel within the organization have access to relevant reports.
  • Client Visibility: Clients can view progress reports and check-in statuses for engineers assigned to their specific projects.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, we may disclose information to proper authorities.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures (including SSL/TLS encryption) to protect your data during transmission. Access to the platform is restricted via password-protected authentication using role-based access controls (RBAC).

5. Your Rights

Users have the right to request access to their profile and data records. If you believe your data is incorrect, you may update your profile or contact the system administrator to correct any information.
